About

OurStick is made by Giorgos, in Greece.

It started with my brother-in-law's external disk. It failed, and I rescued what was on it: about 900 GB of photos, music and videos, many years of them. Then I had to give it all back to him. A cloud service meant a subscription and days of uploading, only to download it all again. A USB stick big enough meant buying one and taking it to him.

What I wanted was simple: put the folder on a stick, hand it over, and have nothing in between that could read it. So I made a stick that travels as a link.

OurStick is free and open source. Questions, bugs, ideas: hi@ourstick.com.
