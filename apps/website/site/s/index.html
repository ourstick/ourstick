Someone sent you a stick.

Open it with OurStick, a free app for your computer.

OurStick is for computers. There is no phone app yet: open this link on a Mac, a Windows or a Linux computer.

Already have OurStick?

Copy it, then open OurStick and choose “I got a sharing link” (or “Add a sharing link”): it finds the copied link by itself. Or copy the address from your browser's address bar.

The installers are not signed yet. On Windows, your browser may warn that the file is not commonly downloaded (choose Keep), and its first start may say “Windows protected your PC”: choose More info, then Run anyway.

Everything in the link after “#”, the stick's key and where to find the sender, is never sent by your browser to any website, this one included. It stays in your browser's history, like every address you open.
