Someone sent you files

They come straight from their computer to yours. To get them, you need the free OurStick app.

  1. Get OurStick and open it.

    Download for Mac Apple silicon (M1 and later), macOS 11 or newer · SIZE_MACOS Download for Windows 64-bit · SIZE_WINDOWS Download for Linux Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 or newer · SIZE_LINUX

    Already have it? Skip to step 2.

  2. Copy the link, then press Receive in OurStick. It finds the link by itself.

On a phone? OurStick is only for computers for now. Open this message on your computer.

This link is not whole.

Part of it was lost on the way, maybe cut off by the program it was sent with. Ask the sender to send it again.

The Mac app is signed and notarized by Apple. The Windows installer is not signed yet: your browser may warn that the file is not commonly downloaded (choose Keep), and its first start may say “Windows protected your PC”: choose More info, then Run anyway.

This page never sees the files or the key: they stay in the link, on your device. Everything after “#” is never sent by your browser to any website, this one included; it stays in your browser's history, like every address you open.
