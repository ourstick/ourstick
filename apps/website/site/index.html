OurStick

A USB stick you can send.

Put folders on a stick, send its link or QR code, and the other person sees and downloads the files, as if you handed them a USB stick.

Straight from your computer to theirs, encrypted on your device. No accounts, no uploads to a cloud, no size limits. When you add files, the people who have your stick get them too.

The installers are not signed yet. On Windows, your browser may warn that the file is not commonly downloaded (choose Keep), and its first start may say “Windows protected your PC”: choose More info, then Run anyway.

How it works

OurStick makes a folder on your computer that works like a USB stick. Drop files in it, and share its link. Whoever opens the link with OurStick chooses what to get and downloads it from you, directly when your computers can reach each other, or through a public relay when they cannot. Everything is encrypted before it leaves your computer, and the key is in the link: only people you send it to can read the stick.

Your computer has to be on for others to get your stick.
